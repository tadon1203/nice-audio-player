# 0005: All motion is a spring, with three exceptions

Superseded by [0006](./0006-three-durations-and-one-spring-for-interruptible-motion.md), then by [0007](./0007-one-spring-curve-generated-in-house.md). The current rules are in 0007.

Every movement was a fully damped spring (`bounce: 0`). The exceptions were press (`bounce: 0.25`), reduced motion (a 100ms crossfade), and progress motion (constant speed).
